Registry Scope

This index provides access to Commercial Litigation Registry records by country, legal system and selected sub-jurisdiction. Country pages provide the primary national overview. Where a country contains commercially significant internal legal systems, provinces or states, dedicated sub-jurisdiction records provide a more specific entry point.

Use the country-level page for an overall legal-system and cross-border view. Use a sub-jurisdiction page where the relevant court structure, procedure, governing law, commercial centre or enforcement path is materially linked to that state, province or legal system.
